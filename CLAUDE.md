# Copilot - Parent Co-Pilot App

## Purpose & Context

Nick is pursuing dual objectives: building a working end-to-end AI-powered application while documenting the learning process for content creation and showcasing technical mastery. The specific problem he's addressing is personal—becoming more proactive in planning for his children rather than only responding when his wife (who currently handles all planning responsibilities) explicitly tells him what needs to be done. Success means having conversations about upcoming needs before his wife has to bring them up, essentially building the same mental models she has developed through experience.

The project, dubbed "Copilot," is conceived as an AI-powered mental model builder for co-parents that would capture the primary planner's thinking and surface context proactively to help the less-informed parent develop anticipation skills over time. Nick works as a Product Manager and brings expertise in that domain, seeking to expand his capabilities in AI agents, design, and engineering through this hands-on project.

## Current State

Nick has completed the conceptual foundation and planning phase. He's developed a comprehensive Product Requirements Document that defines the MVP scope around three core features:
1. A brain dump interface for the primary planner
2. Daily digests for the secondary parent
3. Contextual modals for deeper understanding

The technical architecture recommendations and week-by-week development plan are in place.

The most critical immediate step identified is validating the concept with his wife before beginning development, as her reaction will determine whether they're solving the right problem or need to pivot the approach.

## Key Learnings & Principles

A significant reframing occurred during the conceptual phase: the real issue isn't needing reminders or task execution, but rather not being "in the loop" and lacking the mental models that the primary planner has built through experience. This insight shifted the solution from a simple reminder system to a mental model builder that helps develop anticipation skills over time.

The core principle emerged that primary planners aren't superhuman—they've simply built mental models through repetition, and technology can help transfer those models to co-parents more efficiently.
